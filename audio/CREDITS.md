## Synthesised

- **From Rogue Guys, through ARC Dungeons:** `cast_fire_1..3`, `cast_cold_1..3`,
  `cast_lightning_1..3`, `swing_1..3` and `dodge_1..2`. Rogue Guys' `tools/gen_sfx.py` made them,
  so they are our own work.
- **Made here:** every `.wav`. `tools/gen_sfx.py` builds them from noise, sines and filters (many
  of its recipes are ARC Dungeons'), so they are our own work too.
